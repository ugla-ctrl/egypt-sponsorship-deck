The Invitation

Meet Totality in Luxor.

On 2 August 2027, the Sun will disappear over the Nile for six minutes and twenty-three seconds. The world will not see a longer totality on land for another eighty-seven years. We are gathering to meet this moment on purpose, with connection, awe, remembrance, discovery, and intentionality.

The Place

In the City of the Sun.

Luxor, one of the oldest continuously inhabited places on Earth, was built over the ruins of Thebes, capital of Egypt during the New Kingdom. We will view the eclipse from private land on the West Bank of the Nile, near the Colossi of Memnon, the Valley of the Queens, and the temple of Hatshepsut, Egypt's famous female pharaoh.

The Programming

What fills the days between monuments.

Not a traditional festival. Every day weaves together the ancient and the speculative: music, art, a speaker series, health and wellness, held at a human scale and led by the people in the room.

On the River

Temple tours at dawn, talks while sailing the Nile, music in the evenings.

Heritage

Egyptologist-led visits to Karnak, Luxor Temple, Edfu, Kom Ombo, Philae, and Abu Simbel.

Ceremony & Wellness

Yoga and breathwork, shared meals, rest, and ceremony around totality.

Music on the Land

Local Egyptian musicians and international DJs after totality on the West Bank.
