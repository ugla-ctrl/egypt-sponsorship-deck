The world will not see a longer totality on land for another eighty-seven years. We are gathering to meet this moment on purpose with connection, awe, remembrance, discovery, and intentionality. We inherited this alignment, and we gather to design the future with intention. Inquire V · The Questions Before you choose a way in Frequently asked questions, answered plainly. Is pricing per person or per room? Per person. Every guest needs their own ticket, and accommodation is priced per person. The more who share a room or apartment, the less each person pays. We're a couple or a group. How do we book together? Each person books a place in the same room or apartment, and we room you together. Book the matching option for each traveller and let us know you're a group. For a room or a whole apartment to yourselves, choose a Private option. What's included? Every accommodation pathway includes the full Luxor Eclipse Gathering (New Moon ceremony 1 August, eclipse viewing and celebration 2 August, send-off brunch 3 August), all meals, ceremonies, temple visits, and transport to and from the grounds. The cruise adds seven nights aboard, all tours, and resident Egyptologists. The Gathering pass on its own is for those who already have a place to stay. Does my package include the Gathering? Yes. Every accommodation pathway (cruise, Marsam, Omda) includes full Gathering access. Can I pay over time? Yes. Pay in full, or in equal monthly installments at no added cost: no interest, no fees, the same price either way, open worldwide. Depending on your country, a wider range of financing options may be available at checkout. What's the deposit, and is it refundable? A refundable deposit holds your place while we confirm your booking. It is fully refundable, and it is applied toward whichever pathway you choose. What if there's unrest or conflict in the region? Your safety comes first. If conditions make the gathering unsafe to hold, our force-majeure terms apply: a credit toward a future gathering, or a refund, as set out in the Terms and Conditions. Can I add or upgrade later? Yes. Secure your place now, then add an extension or move to a longer pathway later. Write to marhaba@egypt-eclipse.com. Can I choose who I room with? Yes. A rooming request opens ahead of the gathering, where you can tell us who you'd like to be placed with. Solo bookers of a shared option get paired with another traveller. The cost is a stretch for me. We keep a small number of places open through our scholarship and energy-exchange program. Write to marhaba@egypt-eclipse.com and tell us what you'd bring. You can also save your application without paying a deposit while you decide. Where do I fly into? Most international flights arrive via Cairo. From there, you continue to Aswan for the cruise, or to Luxor for hotel stays and the gathering. Our team will arrange transportation to and from the airport for you. Where will we view the eclipse? Around Luxor. We're holding private land on the West Bank, near the Colossi of Memnon. We'll share the exact site as the gathering approaches. Is the 36-hour Gathering continuous? Activities run from the evening of 1 August through the morning of 3 August, with totality on the 2nd. You're welcome to come and go. How do I get to the gathering each day? We provide shared transportation to and from the gathering grounds, running from set locations in Luxor. Pick-up points will be announced closer to the gathering. Omda stays include dedicated transport, and the Marsam is a short walk from the grounds. What food will be served? All meals and refreshments are included throughout, with fresh Egyptian and international cooking. We'll ask about your dietary needs ahead of the gathering so the kitchen can take care of you. Is it safe to travel to Egypt? Yes. Luxor and the Nile Valley are among the most visited places in Egypt, and we work with trusted local partners on the ground. Our team handles transport, accommodation, and the gathering site, and we'll send detailed travel guidance before you go. What about the heat? August in Luxor is hot and dry, and the gathering is designed around it. Activities are held in the cooler mornings and evenings, with shaded places to rest and water and refreshments throughout the day. Is this a festival like Iceland? Not quite. Each eclipse gathering takes its shape from where it is held: local customs, how remote the site is, the time of year. Egypt is not a traditional festival, though there is content woven into every part of the trip: music, art, a speaker series, health and wellness. The Luxor Eclipse Gathering is the heart of it, and every pathway includes it. Totality on the 2nd, then music into the evening on the land we are holding on the West Bank, a mix of local Egyptian musicians and international DJs. Around that, each pathway has its own rhythm. On the river, temple tours in the morning, yoga and breathwork after, talks while sailing the Nile, and music in the evenings. Wherever you stay, expect ceremony, shared meals, and time to rest, held at a human scale. What is the Trilogy? Three total eclipses across three summers: Iceland 2026, Egypt 2027, Australasia 2028. The Trilogy Pass holds a place in all three gatherings, walked in order with the people who choose the whole arc. Can I book my own place to stay? Yes. The Luxor Eclipse Gathering pass is made for this: full access to the gathering, meals and refreshments included, for those who already have a place to stay in Luxor. Is Giza single or double occupancy? Shared hotel, double occupancy, priced per person. A private room is available on request via marhaba@egypt-eclipse.com. Domestic flights within Egypt are included when Giza is booked with a Luxor package. How do I get to Giza? The Giza Activation runs 6 to 9 August, after the gathering in Luxor.
